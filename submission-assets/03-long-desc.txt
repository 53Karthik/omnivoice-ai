OmniVoice AI brings text chat, browser voice dictation, and spoken responses into one interface. Upload readable PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, CSV, or Excel files and ask questions grounded in retrieved document excerpts. Manage conversations in your browser, toggle document context, and export chats as Markdown. Chat history is stored locally in your browser. A configured Gemini API key is required. Voice support varies by browser; scanned PDFs require OCR, which is not included.
